About

Health · Behavior · Development

Dr. Ari Bronsoler Nurko is a behavioral economist at Google, where he applies economic principles and behavioral insight to drive positive change.

His doctoral research at MIT examined how technology and decision-making shape health outcomes, with the aim of making healthcare more efficient and higher quality, particularly in developing nations. He designs large-scale interventions that combine data analytics, technology adoption and organizational improvement.

He has worked with IMSS, Mexico’s largest public healthcare provider, and with private providers on programs for diabetes management, preventive care and heart attack treatment. His peer-reviewed work appears in the Review of Economic Studies, the Annual Review of Economics and NEJM Catalyst.

The thread running through all of it is accessible, equitable healthcare, and the power of health information technology to create lasting impact.
